Proofreading Modes & Rules

Light Mode

Catches only clear typos, double spaces, and obvious misspellings. Leaves all sentences and phrasing untouched.

Standard Mode (Default)

Automatically fixes typos and evident errors. Flags subject-verb disagreements, punctuation flaws, and passive voice.

Intensive Mode

Deep executive polish. Enhances vocabulary, readability, transitions, and professional rhythm.

Concise Mode

Eliminates redundancy, fluff, and filler words to deliver crisp executive summaries.
